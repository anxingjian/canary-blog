# The Interoceptive Turn: Self from Within
**Author:** Noga Arikha  
**Source:** Aeon (June 17, 2019)  
**Date Read:** 2026-06-13  
**Length:** ~4,300 words

## Core Argument
The science of interoception — how we sense our bodies from within — is dismantling the Cartesian belief that mind can be understood apart from body. This "interoceptive turn" represents a paradigm shift: consciousness is not produced by a disembodied brain but emerges from constant brain-body interaction.

## Key Concepts

### Interoception
- **Definition:** The perception and integration of all signals from within our body (breathing, heartbeat, temperature, digestion, hormonal states, etc.)
- **Core insight:** Interoception lies at the foundation of self; physiology and mental life are dynamically coupled
- **Historical roots:** Charles Sherrington (1906) coined "interoceptive"; Claude Bernard's "milieu intérieur" (mid-1800s); homeostasis concept (1926)

### The Brain-Body Loop
- **Allostasis:** The process of achieving stability through regulation *in response to* predicted environmental changes — not just maintaining a static state
- **Prediction:** The brain continuously generates predictions about sensory information based on past experience, testing them against interoceptive signals
- **Homeostatic regulation:** The ANS (autonomic nervous system) constantly adjusts in feedback loops with the brain

### Selfhood Arises from Embodied Interaction
- **Anterior insula:** Brain region involved in interoceptive processing, body ownership, emotional experience, and empathy
- **Interoceptive accuracy:** The ability to detect bodily states (e.g., heartbeat detection) correlates with:
  - Self-stability (resistance to Rubber Hand Illusion)
  - Capacity for empathy
  - Emotional self-knowledge
  - Character traits

### Embodied Mentalization
- From infancy, the self is shaped through embodied interactions with caregivers (especially affective touch)
- Self-other boundary formation depends on interoceptive ability
- "It is in relation to others that we acquire a sense of self...from infancy on"
- Psychiatric pathologies can result from badly formed self-other boundaries

### Predictive Coding Framework (Friston & Seth)
- Brain is a "statistical organ" that generates predictions about sensory information
- Actions serve to reduce "prediction error"
- Stability of self ensured by "allostatic prediction" — constantly revised engagement with the world
- This is not deterministic; stability is dynamic, never given

## Historical Context: From Descartes to Embodiment

**The Cartesian Error:** Descartes sundered consciousness from body by positing:
- Mind as immaterial "thinking thing"
- Body as mere mechanism
- Animals as lacking mindedness

**Alternatives that persisted:**
- Gassendism: Maintained the continuum of nature
- Vitalism (18th century): Soul inherent in body
- Psychosomatic medicine attempts

**The resurgence:** William James (1890, 1884) argued emotions are *bodily responses first, then interpreted as feelings* — consciousness as continuous stream of embodied experience

**Modern shift:** Antonio Damasio (*Descartes' Error*, 1994) showed embodied emotion integrated with rational processes; 6-fold increase in interoception publications over the past decade

## Philosophical Implications

1. **Beyond Cartesianism:** "The body is the foundation of the conscious mind" — a liberating rebuttal to Descartes
2. **Evolutionary continuity:** Consciousness continuous with primitive homeostatic processes in single cells and bacteria
3. **Body serves mind, not vice versa:** Our brains evolved to serve our bodies' survival
4. **The Interoceptive Turn** overcomes substance dualism and restores the mind-body, human-animal continuum that mechanistic philosophy had severed

## Clinical & Practical Implications

### Psychiatric/Neurological Applications
- Interoceptive disruption linked to: psychosis, schizophrenia, autism, attention disorders, dyspraxia, anorexia, alexithymia, depression
- Biofeedback, physical therapy, yoga shown to modulate interoceptive awareness
- Mindfulness practices work through interoceptive mechanisms

### Western Medicine's Shortcoming
- Mechanistically "chops up the body," leaving patients confused about ailments
- Holistic approaches (yoga, psychosomatic medicine) aligned with embodiment science

## Philosophical Lineages

- **Merleau-Ponty's phenomenology:** Cognition depends on body with sensorimotor capacities embedded in world
- **Enactivism (Varela):** Cognition arises through embodied action in the world
- **Contemporary philosophers:** Gallagher, Zahavi, de Vignemont bridge neuroscience and philosophy

## Liberating Vision

The interoceptive turn shows:
- We are not posthuman machines but deeply embodied biological creatures
- Understanding self requires humility: we are "just one aspect of biology"
- Consciousness remains mysterious but grounded in biology, not immaterial
- This enables us to understand social nature (empathy, racial bias) and relational embodiment

**Woolf's window pane:** "No window pane into the self is perfectly transparent. But we are clearing up some smudges."
